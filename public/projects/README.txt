Place your real project screenshots in this folder when available.

Recommended names:
- rent-wise-preview.png (or .jpg)
- retail-data-analysis-preview.png (or .jpg)

To display your screenshot:
1. Copy the image into this folder.
2. In `src/data/portfolioData.js`, add `image: "/projects/rent-wise-preview.png"` to the corresponding project object.
3. The ProjectCard will automatically switch from the architectural schematic to your real screenshot.
